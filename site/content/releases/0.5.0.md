---
version: 0.5.0
date: 2026-10-15
title: Build here, account moves and a clearer chat
intro: You brief Kernel itself now, and a chat with Kernel can hand work to teammates or build it in place, with its own reviewed pull requests. Switching accounts tells you which long chats would re-read their history and what that costs, and lets you keep them where they are or move them with a summary. New chat is a page with the same message box as a chat. Turns fold their steps into one line, and Settings has fewer, clearer pages. Rooms can pin read-only reference folders, @ lists folders and teammates, and Kernel has a new icon.
---

## New

- Settings, a room, Setup and scripts offers a script Kernel found in the repo under an empty Setup or Archive row, with a Use button. (#654)
- A room with no setup script now offers a suggested script, Ask Kernel and Add manually in the Setup tab. (#658)
- Kernel can set a room's setup, run and archive scripts, and saves them only after you check them on a card and press Save. (#682)
- When you tell the Lead to go past a review's blockers, Kernel remembers it for that commit, so Merge works without Merge anyway until the next commit, and auto-merge still waits. (#694)
- Right-click any image to copy it or save it to a file. (#695)
- You can overrule a code review's blockers from the PR band, so the PR reads ready and merges normally until a new commit brings them back. (#697)
- Typing @ in the message box now also lists folders, and picking one gives the agent the folder's path. (#702)
- A message to the Lead that mentions a teammate tells the Lead who you addressed, and the Lead is reminded to act for each one before it ends its turn. (#707)
- Typing @ in Kernel's chat lists the room's teammates above the files, and picking one adds a chip that the Lead reads as addressed to them. (#710)
- A room can pin folders outside its repo as read-only references, so its agents can read a sibling project and the @ menu searches it. (#713)
- Teammates can ask the Lead a question or propose handing part of their work to another teammate, and both show in the Team update so the Lead can answer or ask you. (#716)
- Settings, room, General lists the room's reference folders, with Add folder and Remove, and marks a folder that is no longer on the Mac as missing. (#721)
- A toast now warns that switching models mid-chat makes the next reply slower and uses more tokens. (#731)
- Under New chat's box you can pick the room, Worktree or Local, and the branch, and the + menu can link a pull request. (#752)
- Chats with Kernel can switch between Hand off and Build here from the composer, with the default for new chats set in Settings, Agents and Room, Agents. (#761)
- A pull request opened from a chat in Build here gets reviewed and can auto-merge like a teammate's, its checks, conflicts and review results come back to that chat, and once it merges the chat's next change starts on a new branch. (#762)
- A Lead chat shows what it still owes above the message box, such as a teammate's question or blockers not passed on, and you can dismiss each one. (#766)
- Agents can draw Claude Design canvases and share them as links. (#771)
- Control 1 to 9 open the workspaces of the chat you're in. (#805)
- Switching accounts now shows which long chats would re-read their history, what that costs, and lets you keep them on their account or move them, with the matching settings in Settings, Accounts. (#839)
- A long chat now says above the composer when its next turn would re-read its history on another account or model, with the size and, on an API key, the price, and lets you continue with a summary, continue anyway or keep it on its account. (#842)
