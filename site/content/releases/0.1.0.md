## Highlights

- **Statuses from real events.** Every status on your team comes from a real agent event, never a timer.
- **Plans you approve.** The lead splits a brief into tasks and hands each one out.
- **Workspaces.** One git worktree, branch and port for every task.
- **Inbox.** Plans, risky commands and questions in one place.
- **Pull requests, end to end.** Create, fix checks, address review and merge.
- **Checkpoints** after every turn, and your agent's own terminal in a tab.
- **Settings and a light theme,** plus macOS notifications.

## Under the hood

- Signed and notarized by Apple, with automatic updates. (#32)
- Hooks stay silent when Kernel isn't running. (#33)
- Open at login is off by default, and only the installed app can turn it on. (#34)
- A smaller download, with unused native files left out. (#35)
- Repos without a remote, or with a master branch, work out of the box. (#37)
- Every release checks that the app's native modules load before it ships. (#38)
