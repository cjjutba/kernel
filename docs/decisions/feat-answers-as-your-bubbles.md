**A bubble on the right means you said or clicked it, and Kernel's own messages are never your bubble.** A settled plan card is followed by your answer drawn from the approval: "Approved", or the notes from Request changes. A question card keeps its questions and your answers are the bubble under it, one line each, with no "You answered:". These bubbles are not sent messages, so they have no Edit or Retry and no checkpoint or chat rail entry. Kernel's message that answers a plan (after a restart, or on Codex) carries `approvalId` and is not drawn, since the approval's bubble stands for it. Tool Allow and Deny lines, "INTERRUPTED BY YOU" and Team update cards are unchanged.

**Kernel's other messages are one note line, like the quit note.** `.note.inline`, a button: clicking it or pressing Enter opens the full text with Copy. A message right after a quit or crash note folds into that note's line, so a relaunch is one line, not two. An expired plan says why from `expiredBy`: "Replaced by a newer plan", "Chat closed" or "Workspace archived", and plain "Expired" when unset.

Request changes images are not kept on the approval yet, so the bubble shows the notes only. Noor keeps them on the approval and the bubble shows them in a follow-up. WorkspacePlanApproved, WorkspaceQuestionAnswers and the plan states in `design/redesign/` still draw the old Plan approved line and "You answered" card, and the app now differs from them on purpose.
