**A paste becomes a chip over 10 lines or 1,000 characters.** D-062 made it a chip over 4 lines or 280 characters. CJ found that too tight: a short paragraph, or a list of five bullets, turned into `pasted_text_N.txt` when it was easier to read and edit in the box. The box shows 7 lines before it scrolls, so a paste up to 10 lines still fits with a little scrolling, and 1,000 characters is two short paragraphs. Longer pastes, such as logs, stack traces and files, still become chips. Amends D-062.
