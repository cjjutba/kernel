**The rest of the screens follow the KERNEL-919 contract.** Issues keep a list spot (`issues:<projectId>`) and a detail spot per issue (`issues:<projectId>:<issueId>`). History keys by its project filter (`history:<projectId>` or `history:all`), Projects keeps one (`projects`), the sidebar keeps `sidebar`, and Settings keeps one per page, project and section through `spotKey.settings`, plus `settings:app:nav` for its nav. A Settings route `row` or an `agentId` editor is a target and skips the saved spot, and the agent editor saves under its own key so it can't overwrite its page's spot.
