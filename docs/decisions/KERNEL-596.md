**Archiving the chat or workspace on screen goes to the room's front door, never to History or another chat.** Replaces KERNEL-132's rule that an archive by hand lands in History, and amends KERNEL-114 and KERNEL-338, which opened the room's top chat or the chat above the archived one. Two rules decided the landing and raced. PrHeader's layout effect sent a workspace archived by hand to History, and `archiveChatNow` tried to open the neighbouring chat first. KERNEL-578's capture passed only because it simulated main. Real main archives a chat's workspaces one by one, so History won a frame, and the maintainer saw History flash and then another chat open. The maintainer chose a neutral home after an archive.

The rule has one function, `archiveDestination` in `nav.ts`. If the route is a workspace that was just archived, it answers `frontDoor` for that workspace's room, which is New chat with Kernel, or Team when the room has no Lead. For any other route it answers nothing, so archiving something that is not on screen leaves you where you are. `frontDoor` is not `roomHome`: it never picks a chat, so the destination is the same whatever else is open in the room.

`followArchives` in `archiveLanding.ts` applies it for the life of the app. It is a store listener, so the archived status and the move are one synchronous step. No frame shows the workspace stripped of its tabs, and nothing waits for main to answer, which is after its pushes. A failed archive never stores the status and never moves you, and the caller shows the error. Every path uses it: the chat row's Archive, closing a chat's last tab, ConfirmArchiveChat, the banner's archive, ConfirmCloseChats, a workspace row's Archive, the PR header's Archive and ConfirmArchive, a review archived once its work merged, and Kernel archiving a workspace after its PR merged. The shared Lead workspace from before KERNEL-405 has no archive, so `closeChats` calls the same function when its last chat closes.

A workspace Kernel archives on its own keeps its toast, since it was not your click: a merged PR, or a review whose work merged or closed. An archive by hand has none (KERNEL-495). The motion of KERNEL-579 and KERNEL-580 stays, fading to the front door. In a room with open chats no sidebar row stands for New chat, so only the room's Lead row, shown while no chat is open, takes the selected fill. KERNEL-596.
