**The window can't zoom below 100%, and every zoom moves the traffic lights onto the top row.** macOS draws the traffic lights at a fixed size, so page zoom leaves them where the window was made. Kernel now places them for each zoom at x round(20z) and y round(31z - 7), which centers the 14pt buttons on the top row's line at y 31z. A capture on macOS 27 measured the buttons 14 tall and the three of them 60 wide. Zooming out had to stop where the lights still fit in the hidden sidebar header's 78z of padding with an 8 gap, so 20z + 60 + 8 ≤ 78z, which needs z of at least 1.17. No step below 100% passes, so ⌘-, View, Zoom Out and a pinch stop at 100%, and a saved zoom below it goes back to 100% at launch. At 100% the lights end at 80, past the 78 of padding, but the sidebar toggle at 87 still clears them by 7. To allow zooming out again, the header padding and the toggle would have to stop shrinking with the page (KERNEL-901).
