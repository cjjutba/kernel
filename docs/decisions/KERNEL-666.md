**Corners follow one scale in `tokens.css`: `--r-2xs` 4px, `--r-xs` 6px, `--r-sm` 8px, `--r` 10px, `--r-md` 12px, `--r-lg` 16px, `--r-xl` 18px and `--r-pill` 999px.** The corners are softer to sit next to the Hugeicons glyphs. A control's radius is about 0.35 times its height, so 28 to 34px controls take `--r`, 22 to 26px controls and rows take `--r-sm`, and 18 to 20px marks take `--r-xs`. An inner radius is never larger than its outer one: the outer radius minus the padding, and `calc(<outer token> - 1px)` for halves inside a 1px border. No renderer CSS hard-codes a px radius, only `50%`, `0`, `inherit` and a token or a `calc()` around one. SVG `rx` values in glyphs are drawing geometry and are exempt. KERNEL-666.

**The `design/screens/` PNGs and `design/canvas/` markup still show the old radii, and that difference is expected.** `pnpm shots:compare` will show slightly rounder corners than the canvas on every screen until the canvas is redrawn to this scale. Don't "fix" a corner back to match a PNG. KERNEL-666.
