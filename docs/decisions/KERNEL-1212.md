**The bottom pane is a drawer under the chat, not a pane of the right panel.** Amends KERNEL-512 and KERNEL-513, which split the right panel into a top pane and a bottom pane. Setup, Run and Terminal now sit in a drawer the width of the chat column, below the composer, with a hairline above it. The right panel keeps its tabs (PRs, All files, Changes, Checks) and the PR band, and uses the whole panel height. The terminal was squeezed to the panel's width, which wrapped long lines and made a build log hard to read. Under the chat it has the width of the chat and the transcript above it.

**The title bar's first toggle and ⌘J open the drawer, and nothing else.** They no longer open the right panel first, since the drawer doesn't live there. The toggle reads "Show terminal" and "Hide terminal", and is pressed while the drawer is open. Opening Setup from a chat's setup rows or a failed setup toast, and the Run shortcut, open the drawer on that tab and leave the right panel as it was. KERNEL-1212.

**The drawer keeps the size and fold rules of the bottom pane.** Its height is still one fraction shared by every chat, 1/3 until dragged, saved as `kernel.bottomPanelRatio`, now a fraction of the chat column's height. The hairline above it is the handle. The chat keeps at least 320px for the transcript and composer, and the drawer at least its tab bar plus 120px. Collapsed or open is still saved apart for Kernel's chats (start closed) and teammate workspaces (start open), and a collapsed bar keeps the Run button and says what runs. The space at the right of the tab strip is left free for terminal actions. Split terminals, a + for more terminals and a close button are not part of this change. KERNEL-1212.
