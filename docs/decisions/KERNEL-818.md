**Kernel's logo is the Block K, and `design/brand/` holds its masters.** The node K (a K built from three circles on lines) is retired. The Block K is a stem, a channel and a chevron. The chevron's edges run at a 1:2 slope and meet in a short flat face opposite the stem, so the K is all straight lines and two angles, which hold up at 16 px where the node K's small circles turned to mush. `design/brand/k.py` draws every SVG from one construction, and `design/brand/build.mjs` renders the PNGs and the review sheet (`design/brand/sheet.png`). Edit the numbers in `k.py` and run both, never a file by hand. Everything in `design/site/logo/` is an export of these masters and keeps its old file name.

The geometry has two cuts. The icon K (`k-icon.svg`) sits on a 100 x 96 grid: stem 32, channel 14, chevron 34 wide measured on a horizontal (30.4 across the stroke, a touch under the stem so the diagonals look as heavy as the vertical), flat face 16, outer corners rounded at r 2.5 and inner corners sharp. The wordmark K (`k-wordmark.svg`) is drawn in Inter units at Inter's cap height of 1490: stem 267 (Inter Semibold's own stem), channel 96, chevron 284, flat face 248, corners at the same proportion as the icon. Round 2 had a 150 channel. At 18 px that is 1.3 px, and wherever it landed on a whole pixel it left a clean light column, so the word read as "I<ernel". At 96 the channel is 0.84 px and never opens wider than the gap between the K and the e (209 units, 1.8 px at 18). The icon K keeps its 14 channel, because at icon sizes the channel is what makes it the Block K.

The app icon (`icon-tile-1024.svg`) follows Apple's grid: a 1024 canvas, an 824 squircle at 100, 100 with continuous corners at r 160. The tile is flat #09090a, the app's dark canvas token, with no gradient on the tile. Its edge is a white hairline at 16% opacity, drawn as a 2 px stroke clipped to the tile so 1 px shows. The K spans 68% of the tile's width (560 px) and is centered vertically. Horizontally it moves 2.83 grid units (15.9 px at 1024) right of its box center: the chevron's notch puts the ink's mass left of center, and the shift is half the gap between the box center and the ink's centroid. The glyph carries the only gradient, a vertical sheen over the K's own height from #ffffff at the top to #c8c8cc at the bottom. No shadows.

The 16 and 32 px icons are separate files drawn on the pixel grid, `icon-tile-16.svg` and `icon-tile-32.svg`, with a flat white K and no sheen. Scaled down from 1024, the channel blurs shut. At 32 the tile is 26 px at 3, 3 (r 5) and the K is 16 tall: stem 5, channel 2, face 2, chevron 6, hairline 22%. At 16 the tile is 13 px at 1.5, 1.5 (r 2.5) and the K is 8 tall: stem 3, channel 1, face 2, chevron 3, hairline 20%. Any icon export at 16 or 32 uses these files: `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon.svg` (the 32 file, since a Retina tab draws a 16 pt favicon at 32 pixels), and the 16 and 32 entries of the app's `.icns`. 64 and up scale from the 1024 file. The Apple touch icon fills its square with the tile's contents and no hairline, because iOS draws its own mask.

Use the wordmark K only inside the lockup (`wordmark-ink.svg`, `wordmark-white.svg`), where it sits beside "ernel" in Inter 600 at -0.02em. Use the icon K everywhere the K stands alone: the app icon, favicons, `mark-black.svg` and `mark-white.svg`, and the in-app mark. Ink is #18191b and white is #f7f7f8, the app's two ink tokens. The lockup's smallest size is 14 px (font size, so the cap height is 10 px). The bare mark's smallest size is 10 px tall.

In the app, Kernel's own mark (the Lead's avatar, the Welcome screen and Home, today `KernelMark` and `LargeMark` in `icons.tsx`) moves from the Hugeicons layers glyph to the icon K, filled in `currentColor`. The layers glyph is a stock icon that means "stack" in other apps and has nothing to do with Kernel, so the app's mark never matched the Dock icon, the site or the DMG. With the K the same letter shows up in the Dock, the installer, the website and the Lead's avatar. The K is solid where the app's other icons are strokes, which is right for an avatar standing in for a letter. Draw it at about 0.8 of the icon box's height and never under 10 px. KERNEL-818
