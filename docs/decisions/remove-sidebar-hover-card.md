**Superseded by kernel-630-sidebar-hover-card.** **Sidebar rows show no hover card.** Amends D-084, KERNEL-336 and KERNEL-339. CJ did not want a card opening beside the sidebar when the pointer rests on a chat, Lead or workspace row. The rows keep what they had: the state icon, the name, the diff totals or PR number, Archive on hover, and the icon's own short tooltip. The row's accessible name still says the state, so nothing the card said is lost to keyboard and screen reader users. `chatWaits` stays, since the folded row's icon follows the same rule. The `--working` and `--tint-*` tokens were only for the card's status pill and are left in `tokens.css` for now. GroupedRowStatus still draws the card in its PNG and canvas, so its shot differs from the PNG by that card alone.
