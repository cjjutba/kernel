**Toasts slide in and out, keep to one line where they fit, and close with a swipe right instead of a close button.** CJ asked for toasts that animate instead of snapping into place, with no x and one line of text where possible, and for a swipe right to close one. WorkspaceToast.png draws a 300px toast with the title over the sub and an x, so the shot now differs from the PNG. The title and sub run on as one line of text, the title in ink and the sub muted, and wrap only when they don't fit. The stack is 360px wide, because at 300px most medium-length toasts still wrapped. A new toast's slot opens over 160ms so the toasts above glide up, then the toast slides in from past the right edge over 340ms. Leaving, it slides back out and fades over 200ms, its slot closes over 160ms, and only then does `onDismiss` take it out of the store. A pointer drag moves the toast with the pointer. A two-finger trackpad swipe does the same through wheel events. Kernel reads those with natural scrolling, the macOS default, where fingers moving right report a negative deltaX, so with natural scrolling off a swipe right does nothing. Let go past 60px, or past 16px while moving faster than 0.3px per ms, and the toast slides on out from where it is. Short of that it springs back. A trackpad swipe ends once its wheel events, momentum included, stop for 120ms. A swipe never moves a toast left of its place. Pressing the action dismisses the toast from inside `Toast`, with the same slide out. Without the x there is no key that dismisses a toast, but every toast still leaves by itself 2.6s after it loses hover and focus. With reduced motion a toast appears and vanishes in place. The update card keeps its close button (KERNEL-164).
