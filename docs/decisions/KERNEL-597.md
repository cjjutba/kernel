**The sidebar's scrollbar is a thin thumb on the column's edge, and the resize handle moves out of its way.** Amends D-112. The scrolling list sat inside the frame's `0 8px 0 10px` padding, so its scrollbar started 8px from the edge and drew Chromium's default track. The app list and the Settings list now share `.sb-scroll` (sidebar.css). It bleeds out to the frame's edges, keeps its own 10px left padding and holds a 6px scrollbar gutter (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`) beside 2px of right padding, so rows end 8px from the edge as before and line up with the rows above whether or not the list scrolls. A scrollbar styled with `::-webkit-scrollbar` takes layout width, which is why the right padding is 8px less its 6px. The thumb is `--line-4`, rounded, with no track, and transparent until the pointer is over the list or the list scrolls. `useScrollIdle` sets `data-scrolling` from the scroll event and clears it a second after the last one. Only `::-webkit-scrollbar` rules are used, since `scrollbar-width` or `scrollbar-color` on the element switches them off. The thumb's colour is a custom property on the list, because Chromium did not repaint a thumb whose only change was a `:hover` rule on the list (it stayed hidden in Settings until something else repainted it). D-112 put the sidebar's `ResizeHandle` in the 8px gutter, which is now where the thumb is, and the handle sits above the list, so it would swallow every press on the thumb. The handle now sits on the panel's left 8px (`right: -8px`), with its glow still on the panel's border. Its width, keyboard use and drag are unchanged. KERNEL-597.
