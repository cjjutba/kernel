**The modal scrim dims the page by 30% and blurs it by 2px, lighter than the canvas.** The canvas and the modal PNGs in `design/screens/` use a 55% black scrim with an 8px blur. In the app that left the page behind a modal nearly black, so the dialog looked like it floated over an empty window. At 30% and 2px the sidebar and the rows behind stay readable as shapes, and the blur keeps their text from competing with the dialog's. Don't drop the blur below 2px: modals have no shadow (DESIGN.md), so the blur and the 1px border are what set the dialog apart from the page. The light theme scrim goes from 28% to 14% to match. Modal screens now differ from their PNGs in the scrim only.
