**The updater keeps checking after an update is ready, and Restart to update waits for a newer download.** A check that finds a newer version downloads it behind the ready one. The pill and Settings, About keep the ready version until the newer one finishes, then show the newer one. Squirrel.Mac installs whatever it last staged, so pressing Restart to update while the newer version downloads waits for it instead of installing the old one. If that download fails or stalls, the ready version installs anyway, because Kernel has already stopped for the restart and leaving it stopped is worse. A plain quit during the newer download still installs the staged older version: Squirrel does that on quit and Kernel can't stop it.

A failed check or download retries after 5 minutes, then 15, then every hour. A check with no update or a finished download resets the backoff and goes back to the 4 hour timer. A download with no progress event for 10 minutes is cancelled through the check's cancellation token, because electron-updater otherwise keeps waiting on the stalled download and ignores the retry. Waking the Mac checks when the last check is at least 30 minutes old.

electron-updater's logger writes through `console.warn`, since Kernel's log only keeps warnings and errors. That puts its info lines, staging percentage and user ID included, in the log. KERNEL-711
