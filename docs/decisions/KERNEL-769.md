**The right panel's toggle stays in the window's top right corner, open or hidden.** Replaces KERNEL-425's placement. With the panel open the toggle ended the chat header, left of the panel's edge, and with it hidden it moved to the window's edge, so a second click missed it. Now it ends the panel's head (`.ws-aside-head`) while the panel is open and ends the chat header while it is hidden, with the same right edge (10px) and vertical centre (22px) in both.

KERNEL-425's reason still holds: inside the coloured PR band the toggle took the band's green, violet or red and read as part of the PR state. So the head is the band (`PrHeader spread` or `LeadPrSummary spread`), a hairline, then a neutral cell that holds the toggle in the same grey as the chat header. The band owns the tone and its bottom line; the cell never takes either. The toggle comes after the band's actions in keyboard order. The cell costs the band 49px, so at the panel's 320px minimum the band is 270px wide, enough for Merge, Open and the chevron. KERNEL-769.
