**A turn shows its steps while it works and folds them when it ends. Amends KERNEL-784, KERNEL-800 and the collapsed-fold decision (nothing-from-inside-a-fold-shows).** Folds are open while their turn runs and close to one summary row on a real result, a stop, or an app restart that cut the turn off, never on a timer. A failed turn stays expanded with its error card. A turn waiting on an approval, a question or a plan stays open. The reader's own toggle always wins: a fold they opened or closed keeps that, per chat.

**The header is the summary alone, and the timer has its own line.** Running: "Read 2 files, ran 2 commands, running 1 more". Done: "Ran 5 commands, read 3 files", with the red failed or denied tail. The duration is not in the header, since the time line under the reply shows it. A fold of only messages or thinking has no summary, so it reads "Working" while it runs. While a turn runs, one line sits at the end of the transcript, under every fold, card and note, even before the first step: the spinner and the time since the turn's message. While an approval, a question or a plan waits on the reader, the same line reads "Waiting for you", with no spinner and no clock. That comes from the real pending approvals, never a timer. The line goes when the turn ends. (First written as a clock inside the first fold's header, "Working · 43s"; moved out so the timer isn't part of a tool call.)

**A running fold lists its newest 8 steps.** A row at the top ("12 earlier steps") opens the rest and stays open. The newest step of a running fold, when it is a message, streams in full Markdown, so the final reply is already on screen when the fold closes and becomes the reply block under it. This replaces the earlier rule that text stayed hidden until the end.

**The fold waits for the reader.** If they are scrolled away from the end, or hold a selection inside the fold, it stays open until they are back and the selection is gone. A turn under 3 seconds, or of one step, folds without the closing animation. Chats opened after a turn ended show it folded.

**Settings, Appearance, Tool calls** has three choices: "Show while working, fold when done" (default), "Always folded" (folds start closed, even while running) and "Always show" (folds start open and never fold on their own). It replaces the old `toolsOpen` flag, is kept in localStorage like the Changes view, and the other two modes run none of the auto-fold logic. Written without a Linear issue, which was at its free issue limit.

**Long commands end in an ellipsis at the column edge.** The fold's grid has one `minmax(0, 1fr)` column and the rows under it can shrink, so a command longer than the thread column is cut with "…" and the full text shows when the row opens.
