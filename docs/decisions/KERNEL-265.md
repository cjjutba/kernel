**A review takes the branch the Lead passes to create_workspace.** With `review_of` set, Kernel used to drop `branch` and always name the review itself. When that name failed on a long reviewed branch, the Lead had no way around it except `base_ref`, which left the review unlinked (KERNEL-299). Now a passed `branch` goes through the same steps as a task's: cut to 60 characters, checked with `validBranchName`, and given `-2`, `-3` by `freeBranch` when taken. With no `branch`, a review is still `review/<key>` or `review/<a few words of the reviewed title>` (KERNEL-275). The folder and the cleanup after a failed `worktree add` are KERNEL-267 and KERNEL-276. Amends KERNEL-275 and D-126. KERNEL-265.
