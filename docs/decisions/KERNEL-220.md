**Pause near the limit and the weekly warning work, a lifted limit restarts chats two at a time, and a room can't fan out without end.** Amends D-094 and D-017. D-094 said that with no agent limit, the usage meters and pause near the limit are what guard the plan. Nothing read either switch. Kernel paused only once a limit rejected, and when it lifted `carryOn` restarted every cut-off chat at once.

The hold. `nearLimit` in `src/shared/usage.ts` returns the 5-hour or weekly window that holds teammates: utilization at 90% (5-hour) or 95% (weekly), while its reset time is ahead. A warning Claude Code flags with no number counts as close, and a number is read as the number, because the warning comes early: a 5-hour event at 81%, and in the first days of a week at a high pace. With "Pause new work when close to the limit" on, a teammate's chat starts no new turn while a window holds, and the reason is `limit`. It is in all four gates, `waitsFor`, `drain`, `post` and `carryOn`. Turns that run finish. The Lead's chats are never held, as D-094 has it. Send now goes past the hold, as it goes past the agent limit: the user chose it. Held messages go out when a reading drops under the line or the window resets. `pushLimits` releases queues, and a settings change sends the limits again.

D-017 said nothing polls. A window that holds, like a rejected one, gets a timer at its reset time that sets it allowed with utilization 0, and `checkLimits` runs every 90 seconds while a window is rejected or a hold stands.

The weekly warning. With "Warn me before the weekly limit" on, the weekly window at 80% saves one `system` row in the Inbox and shows one macOS banner, in the background. One per window, kept under `weeklyWarned` by reset time, with a minute of slack since the usage call can read a second off.

Resuming. A lifted limit restarts the chats it cut off two at a time (`RESUME_AT_ONCE`). The next starts when one ends its turn, or when its process ends, since a chat that stopped running isn't counted. Chats a quit cut off (KERNEL-215) restart as before.
