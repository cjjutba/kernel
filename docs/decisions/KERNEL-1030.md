**A rejected usage window stands until it really lifts.** Narrows KERNEL-700 and D-069. A rejection lifts when its reset time passes, or when a reading under 95% arrives at least 2 minutes after the window became rejected (`rejectedAt`). Inside those 2 minutes nothing lifts it, and after them a status in a patch (`allowed`, `allowed_warning`) never lifts it by itself, because another chat's event can be older than the rejection and Claude Code's readings can be a minute old. A later reset time no longer starts a new window while the old one's reset time is ahead. KERNEL-700's rule that the highest reading in a window wins still holds, and a rejected window keeps its highest reading while it stands. KERNEL-1030.

**A limit Kernel had no window for is recorded as a temporary one.** A turn stopped by a limit with no blocking window on its account records a rejected 5-hour window with `provisional` and no reset time, reads that account once, and lifts by the rule above. A restart keeps a rejection with no reset time for 5 hours after `rejectedAt`. `checkLimits` reads every account with a rejected window, in use or not. A rejection is copied to every account on the same login (`sameLogin`), the Claude Code login included, since they share one window. KERNEL-1030.
