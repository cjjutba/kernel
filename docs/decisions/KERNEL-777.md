**A limit only wakes the Lead when nothing carries the teammate on before the reset (KERNEL-777).** A turn a usage limit cut off is a `limit` event, not a plain turn or an error. While Kernel moves the provider to another account, the event waits and goes out with the next update that needs the Lead, and it says the teammate hit the limit and carried on there. It wakes the Lead with the paused line, with the earliest reset in the user's time zone, when every account is at its limit, the switch is off or was undone, an account with room needs a sign-in or Keychain access, or the limit is on the model alone. Account tells Team updates when a move ends waiting or the switch fails, so nothing polls for it. The carry-on is a `limit.lifted` event. It wakes the Lead only when the Lead already heard the teammate was paused, so it can correct what it told the user. Kernel saves which workspaces those are with the rest of its Team update state. A limit and its carry-on that wait in the same update read as one line and wake nobody. A cut-off by Kernel's own quit keeps its crash wording.
