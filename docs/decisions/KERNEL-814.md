**⌃1 to ⌃9 open the workspaces of the chat you're in.** Amends KERNEL-357: workspaces get numbers now, scoped to the current chat, and chats keep ⌘1 to ⌘9. The current chat is the one whose row holds the page in view, the Lead chat you're in or the chat that handed off the workspace you're in, so ⌃2 from a workspace jumps to its sibling. ⌃N opens the Nth workspace in sidebar order (oldest first), folded or not, and landing on a hidden row unfolds its chat through the existing `rowHolder` path. With no current chat (Home, Inbox, Settings, New chat, a workspace no open chat owns) the keys do nothing and leave the page its keypress. They also do nothing with ⌘, ⌥ or ⇧ held, with a modal open or during onboarding, so ^⌘1 to 4 still picks a model. Holding ⌃ shows ⌃N on those rows after the same delay as the ⌘ hints. ⌘⇧N was ruled out because macOS takes ⌘⇧3, 4 and 5 for screenshots. KERNEL-814.
