**A chat in the sidebar folds, and each level under a room steps in 12px.** Amends D-139 and D-132. A Lead chat that started workspaces folds like a room: hovering it or tabbing to it swaps its icon for a chevron, and pressing the chevron hides the workspaces under it or shows them again. The chevron is a button next to the title button, not inside it, so pressing the title still opens the chat. It has `aria-expanded` and the label "Collapse" or "Expand" followed by the title, and a chat with no workspaces has none. `ui.foldedChats` holds the ids of chats folded by hand, kept in localStorage (`kernel.foldedChats`) across launches, and unfolding drops the id. A chat with no workspaces never counts as folded, and nothing unfolds on its own: opening a hidden workspace from the Inbox or ⌘K leaves its chat folded, as a folded room stays folded. While a chat is folded, its icon also counts the pending approvals of the workspaces it hides, so a plan or question waiting out of sight still shows the plan or needs-you icon, and the chat's own icon returns once nothing waits. Running stays the chat's own. The icon comes from real approvals, with no timer. D-132's numbering skips rows a folded chat hides, so the chat after a folded chat takes the next number and ⌘1 to ⌘9 always match a visible row. A folded room keeps today's rule: it still numbers its rows. `sidebarRows` and `slotsFor` take the folded ids, and `openSlot` passes them, so the sidebar and the shortcut still read one helper. Rows also step in less: `.nav-sub` goes from 30px to 20px and a nested workspace from 56px to 32px, so a chat's icon sits 20px in and a workspace's 32px in. SidebarChatFolded.png draws it.
