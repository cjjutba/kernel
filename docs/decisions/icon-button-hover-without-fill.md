**Icon buttons brighten on hover with no fill behind them.** CJ asked for it. The hover fill was a 24 to 28px square around a 13 to 14px glyph, so the square was what you saw, and in a dense row like a message's actions it jumped from icon to icon. Hovering an icon-only button now turns the icon from `--muted` to `--ink`, a bigger step than the old `--ink-2` because nothing else marks the hover. The button keeps its full size, so the click target doesn't shrink, and keyboard focus keeps its outline. A fill still marks state: a pressed toggle (`aria-pressed`) and a button whose menu is open (`aria-expanded`). Buttons with a label, menu items, rows, split-button carets and the filled send and stop circles keep their hover fills, since the fill there marks the whole target and no small glyph sits inside it. The sidebar's archive button already worked this way, so its own override went away.
