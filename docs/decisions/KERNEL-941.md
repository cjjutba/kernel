**A switch happens at once and says nothing. Amends KERNEL-845 and KERNEL-699.** Every entry point (the account menu, Settings, Accounts, the palette, the footer, a banner) switches without the confirmation KERNEL-845 added. The switch keeps chats on the account that holds their cache while it can run them, as KERNEL-843's Keep does, and each chat that has to move follows Settings, Accounts at its next turn. The user's own switch posts no toast, since the account menu already shows where they went. A switch Kernel makes by itself keeps its toast with Undo, and says "is close to its limit" for a switch ahead of one. `accounts.previewSwitch` went with the dialog. KERNEL-941

**The move notice above the composer shows only while a chat waits for the user's choice. Amends KERNEL-841 and KERNEL-917.** That is a chat under the Ask policy, or one moving onto an API key account, which always asks. Every other move follows the policy, summarize first by default, so there is nothing to decide before it and the notice was noise. The idle hint went with it. The note after a move ("Moved to Personal and re-read 310k tokens") stays in the chat. KERNEL-941

**Switch at a limit is on by default for both providers. Amends KERNEL-568.** Kernel moves to the signed-in subscription account with the most room before the account in use hits a limit (KERNEL-844), so the near-limit offer to switch never shows. Settings saves only what the user chose (KERNEL-225), so everyone who never touched the switch gets the new default, and anyone who turned it off keeps it off. KERNEL-941

**A project pauses for a limit only when none of its chats can run.** A provider counts as stopped in a project when its account in use is limited and no open chat there runs on another of its accounts that has room, as a chat kept on its cache's account does. A chat whose own account is limited waits by itself with the queue reason `limit`, rather than start a turn the account refuses. Only an account's 5-hour or weekly rejection holds a chat this way. A model's own limit still lets the turn go, since its banner offers another model. The banner above a kept chat reads the windows of the account it runs on. KERNEL-941
