**A step row's chevron replaces its icon on hover and focus, and no longer sits at the right edge.** This amends KERNEL-198, which put the chevron at the right end of the row. The sidebar already does it the other way: a room or a folding chat turns its own icon into the chevron (KERNEL-207). Step rows now follow it. The icon, or the file icon on a Read, Edit and Write row, and the chevron share one 15px slot. On hover and keyboard focus the icon fades out and the chevron fades in over it, pointing right while the row is closed and down while it is open, and an open row keeps the chevron after the pointer leaves. Both stay in the slot by opacity, never `display`, so nothing moves. A failed row swaps its red icon the same way. A row with nothing to open is disabled and has no chevron, so it keeps its icon and gets no fill. The rows in the folded "N tool calls" list have no icon, so they have no slot: they show the hover fill and no chevron, and their labels stay where they were. With the chevron gone from the right end, the right-hand notes (`+N -N`, duration) line up on the row's edge. The hover fill, the open body and the `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` wiring are unchanged from KERNEL-198. KERNEL-562.
