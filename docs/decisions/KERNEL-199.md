**Each workspace keeps its own tabs in `ui.tabs`, and Back to app returns to the place Settings was opened from.** Back to app used to open Home, ⌘, always opened General, and the open tab was one slot for the whole app that the workspace screen cleared whenever it showed another workspace. Lead chat 3, then Settings, then Back to app landed on Home, and another workspace and back landed on chat 1. Now `ui.tabs` is keyed by workspace id and holds the active tab, the chat the composer is on (`lastChat`) and the open file and diff tabs. `ui.workspace` keeps only what is global: the right and bottom panels, the checkpoints drawer and expanded tool calls. Images and pasted texts stay in the workspace screen, which still resets them per workspace. A workspace's entry is dropped when it is archived or removed, and when its room is removed. `tabOf` picks what a workspace shows: the stored tab while it still exists (an open chat, or a file or diff in the lists), else `lastChat` if still open, else the first open chat. The sidebar highlights a chat when `tabOf` says the workspace shows it, so a file or diff tab open in the Lead's workspace leaves no chat row current (D-139). Settings remembers two things in the store module, neither kept across a restart: `returnTo`, the place before Settings, set only when Settings opens from outside Settings, and `lastSettings`, the page and room of the last Settings route. Every move, whether `go` changed the route or `openTab` changed the tab, ends in one `moved(from, to)`, where they are recorded. Back to app goes to `nearest(returnTo)`, or to the launch route when Settings was the first place. `nearest` falls back in order: the route, then the room's Lead workspace, then the room's Team page, then Home. So a workspace archived while Settings is open sends you to its room's Lead chat, and a removed or hidden room sends you Home, with no error toast. ⌘, opens `lastSettings`, or General when its room page has no room any more. Esc in Settings goes back, with three exceptions. Nothing happens while a modal or menu is open, since they take Esc first. In the search box the first Esc clears the query, and the next leaves. In any other text field Esc does nothing, so it never discards what you were typing. `openLeadChat` now sets the tab first and then navigates, because the tab no longer gets cleared on arrival.
