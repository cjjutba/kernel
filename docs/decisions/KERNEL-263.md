**A Linear "blocked by" makes a started teammate wait, and never holds the brief.** When Rowan hands off a Linear issue without `wait_for`, Kernel reads the issues that block it from Linear's inverse relations. Each one an open teammate is building, with a workspace that isn't a review and hasn't merged, becomes a wait recorded through `setWait` once the brief has gone out. That makes it `held: false`: the teammate starts now, and Kernel sends the rebase message when the blocking PRs merge. CJ chose this over holding the brief, because Linear relations are often loose and a held brief would stall work the blocker doesn't really gate. Rowan's `wait_for` always wins: when Rowan passed one, Kernel adds nothing from Linear, even when everything Rowan named had merged already. A hand-off whose setup failed gets no wait, since its brief hasn't gone out, and `setWait` would hold it. A Linear error, a missing token or no relations leave the hand-off as it was. KERNEL-263.
