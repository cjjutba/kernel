**The scrollbar thumb shows only while an area scrolls, and is gone about a second after.** Amends feat-fade-scrollbars-after-pointer-and, which also showed the thumb when the pointer moved over an area. Moving the mouse across the sidebar or the main content made thumbs flicker on without any scrolling, so `ui/scrollIdle.ts` no longer reacts to `pointermove` and the per-frame lookup of scrollers under the pointer is gone. Scrolling (wheel, keys, touch, a dragged thumb) sets `data-scrolling`, and so does pressing the native scrollbar, which holds it until release because Electron sends no `pointermove` during a thumb drag. A pointer move with no button down only releases a hold whose `pointerup` was missed, which restarts the countdown and never shows a hidden thumb. The thumb clears 800ms after the last scroll and fades out over 200ms (it was 1000ms and 300ms), with the fade in still 150ms. Reduced motion is unchanged: it snaps. The sidebar list and every scroll area in the main content take the one block in `tokens.css`, and none overrides its `transition` or draws its own thumb. feat-scrollbar-thumb-shows-only-on.
