**A collapsed fold shows nothing from inside it, in a running turn or a finished one. Amends KERNEL-784 and KERNEL-800.** KERNEL-784 kept the step in flight outside the fold on a live line with its command, "Thinking" or a message's first line, so command text flashed on screen with every step before it moved into the fold. There is no live line now. A running turn folds every step of a stretch, the one in flight included, and the summary reads in the present tense: "Running a command", then "Ran 4 commands, running 1 more". Thinking or text in flight adds nothing to it. Opening the fold shows the running step as a row with its command.

**A fold of one step is still a fold.** KERNEL-800 drew a lone step or run as it was. A stretch with at least one visible step is now always a group, so a first step reads "Running a command" and then "Ran a command", and the same row counts up as more steps arrive. A fold's id is its stretch's first item before empty thinking is filtered out, so a fold the user opened never closes mid-turn.

**Once a running turn has made a call, what it writes goes in the fold, the final reply included.** The reply appears in one piece when the turn ends. A turn with no call yet still streams its text.

**Stopped turns, and turns an app restart cut off, fold like finished ones.** The stop note stays under the fold, and the last message stays out of it with the actions. A failed turn keeps every item above its error card with Retry. Approvals, questions, plan approvals and notes stay outside folds. Written without a Linear issue, which was at its free issue limit.
