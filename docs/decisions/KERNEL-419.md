**The Settings nav shares the sidebar's width, and resize edges glow.** Amends D-112 and D-113. A wide sidebar snapped back to 236px when Settings opened, because `.set-nav` was fixed. The Settings nav now reads and writes the same `kernel.sidebarWidth`, through `useSidebarWidth` (the width, the window's limit and the commit, moved out of `Sidebar.tsx`), so dragging either one changes both. The two are never mounted together, so each reads the saved width when it mounts. `.set-nav` keeps 236px as its CSS fallback, as `.sidebar` does. A drag in Settings stops at the 200px minimum and never hides the column, since Settings is the only way back to its pages: `ResizeHandle` takes `hideBelow` and `onHide` as optional, and without them letting go below the minimum clamps. The sidebar and the right panel still pass both. Settings draws `.main` with 8px of padding on every side, so its gutter is 16px where the sidebar's is 8px. The Settings handle is 16px wide and reaches 8px past the nav, so its glow still lands on the panel's border, and `.set-nav` no longer clips with `overflow: hidden`. D-113 said the cursor is the only sign that an edge can be dragged, because a hard 1px hairline did not render cleanly. That is superseded: hovering, keyboard focus or dragging any `ResizeHandle` edge (sidebar, Settings nav, workspace right panel) shows a glow on the panel's border, like Linear's. It is a 3px radial gradient of `--ink` at 45% (no hex, no shadow, the same mix in both themes), brightest and about 2px at the middle of the edge, thinning and fading to transparent toward the top and bottom. It stays at the middle and does not follow the cursor. It fades in over 200ms ease-out after an 80ms delay, so crossing the gutter does not flash it, grows from 60% to full height as it does, and fades out over 120ms. Dragging skips the delay. Reduced motion removes the transition. The 2px keyboard focus ring stays, and Escape, double-click, Enter and the arrow keys are unchanged. KERNEL-419.
