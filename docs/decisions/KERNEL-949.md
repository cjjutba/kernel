**The footer and the Usage popover report usage and never switch accounts. Amends KERNEL-674, KERNEL-697 and KERNEL-759.** The footer's usage row shows only the account each provider runs on. The other accounts' chips, their swap icon and tooltip, and the "more" chip are gone. The Usage popover still lists every other signed-in account with its plan, chats and numbers, as a read-only "Other accounts" list instead of Switch buttons. Switching is in the account menu, Settings, Accounts and the command palette. Once a switch happens at once with no confirmation and Kernel switches at a limit by itself (KERNEL-941), a one-click switch in the footer was easy to press by mistake and a third place to do one thing.
