**The dark theme's greys are pure neutral, with wider steps and brighter text.** Every dark surface, line and text token has equal red, green and blue. The old values leaned slightly blue, which read as flat next to Linear and, at the darkest steps, a little violet. Linear's redesign (linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui) cut chrome color out of its surfaces for the same reason, and sampled from its app the layers are #070707, #0f0f0f, #151515 and #1a1a1a. Ours follow that: canvas #070707, panel #0f0f0f, surface #141414, surface-2 #181818, surface-3 #1e1e1e. Text moved up for contrast: ink-2 #e0e0e0, muted #9c9c9c, faint #7a7a7a, so faint now reaches about 4.5:1 on the panel where it was 3.3:1. Status colors (add, del, mod, merged, needs, working, plan, the PR bands) keep their values, since they carry meaning and are not chrome. There is still no brand accent, and primary buttons stay ink on canvas. The light theme is unchanged. KERNEL-431
