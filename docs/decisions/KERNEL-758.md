**Toasts stay on screen for 5s (KERNEL-758).** CJ found 2.6s too short to read a title and sub, or to reach an action like Undo. A toast now leaves by itself 5s after it appears, or 5s after it loses hover and focus. Amends `toast-motion-and-swipe`, which gave 2.6s. Everything else in that decision stands: hover and focus hold a toast, a swipe right or down dismisses it early, and the rise, sink and gap motion keep their timings.
