**Type, weight, layers and motion are design tokens, in the app and on the website, and tests keep them that way.** Amends D-056 and KERNEL-666. An audit found the app's colors, radii and shadows all on tokens, but 24 font sizes, 13 z-index values, 17 durations and 5 curves written out by hand across 28 CSS files, and about 180 inline styles setting type, color and spacing. `tokens.css` now has `--fs-*`, `--lh-*`, `--fw-*`, `--z-*`, `--dur-*` and `--ease-*`, named by role and taken from the values in use, so nothing moved on screen. The plan mode and archive timings that lived in screen CSS (`--plan-dur`, `--archive-*`) moved there too, and `--working`, `--tint-working` and `--tint-merged`, unused since the hover card's colored pill went, and `--tint-add`, unused since the Settings redesign (KERNEL-680), are gone. Inline styles became classes: a screen's own class for one element's spacing, or a utility in `tokens.css` (`.gap-6`, `.t-meta`, `.fw-medium`). The utilities are `!important` because each replaced a style attribute, which beat every rule; without it a screen's selector would undo them. Spacing has no scale: the odd values match the PNGs, and forcing them onto one would shift screens. The duplicated controls became shared ones: `Checkbox`, Button `size="sm"`, IconButton `box`, and one arrow-key helper (`moveInList`) for every menu and picker. Dropdowns are KERNEL-680's `Select`. `test/theme.test.ts` fails on a literal size, weight, unitless line height, layer of 10 or more, duration or curve outside `tokens.css`, on an inline style with literal type, color or spacing, on a raw checkbox, on a `var()` that names nothing, and on an unused token.

The website keeps its own values, since `design/site/` is its spec (blue-leaning greys, Inter, `del` #eb5757, canvas #08090a, radii of 5 to 16px, dark only), but has the same token families and the same rule. Its `@theme` resets the Tailwind scales it used without tokens (containers, z-index, weights, tracking, easing) and names what it uses: page and lead widths, the glow sizes, `z-raise`, `z-nav` and `z-skip`, translucent colors instead of `/n` opacity modifiers, and durations. `site/tests/unit/rules.test.ts` now covers `lib/`, `app/` and `content/`, colors in any notation, arbitrary values in every Tailwind form, spacing off the documented scale, and raw values in `globals.css` outside `@theme`. `test/designParity.test.ts` checks the colors the two share (add and the merged pair) stay equal. KERNEL-681.
