**An item the Lead owes opens when the Team update reaches the Lead, not when the teammate acts.** A question, a proposed hand-off or a review's blockers sits in the update queue while the Lead is busy, sometimes for minutes. Opening the item at the teammate's tool call would remind the Lead at the end of an unrelated turn about something it hasn't read yet, and the reminder for the real update would then be spent. Opening it on delivery also puts it in the tab that got the update, which is another tab of the chat, or another chat, when the tab that handed the work off is closed. An event that never reaches a Lead, because the room has no Lead chat, opens nothing. KERNEL-749

**Items belong to the Lead chat, and a mention to its tab.** Every tab of a Lead chat calls the work its tabs handed off "yours" (KERNEL-337), so any tab's Stop reminds of the chat's questions, blockers and proposals, and the first tab to stop marks them reminded. A mention stays with the tab the user wrote it in, as KERNEL-745 had it, and the user's next message or Stop in that tab still closes it. KERNEL-749

**The reminder goes once per new item, and lists everything still open.** The Stop hook reminds when an item it hasn't listed yet is open, and the reminder lists every open item, old ones too. Items stay open after the reminder until the Lead acts or the user dismisses them, so `list_workspaces` keeps marking them. A reminder for each Stop would loop a Lead that is right to wait, for one that proposed a hand-off and waits for the user's go-ahead. KERNEL-749

**ask_user closes only what its `about` names, and never blockers.** Without `about`, ask_user closes the tab's mentions as it did before. With it, it closes the questions and proposed hand-offs of those workspaces or agents. Blockers close only when the Lead messages the author's workspace, since only the author can fix them. A newer item of the same kind replaces the older one: one question per workspace, one set of blockers per reviewed workspace, one proposal per proposer and proposed agent. An item whose workspace was archived or deleted closes the next time Kernel reads the chat's items. KERNEL-749
