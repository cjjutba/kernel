**Text has five roles with steps you can see, and every tier meets WCAG AA.** Dark ink was #f5f5f5 (17.6:1 on the panel), ink-2 #e0e0e0 (14.5:1) and ink-3 #c4c4c4 (11.0:1). All three read as white, so an unread row did not stand out from a read one. The roles are now ink for emphasis (titles, the current row, unread, the primary button), ink-2 for default text, ink-3 for secondary text, muted for labels and meta, and faint for icons, disabled controls and decoration. Dark is #f5f5f5, #c8c8c8, #aaaaaa, #8e8e8e, #727272. Light is #18191b, #35383f, #4c4f56, #62666d, #7b7f88. `test/theme.test.ts` holds three rules. Each tier has at least 1.35x the contrast of the next, on every surface. ink, ink-2, ink-3, muted and the status text colors reach 4.5:1 on every surface, hover and surface-3 included. Faint reaches 3:1 and is never body text, which also fixes the old dark faint, 4.47:1 on the panel and 3.88:1 on surface-3. Surfaces, lines and every status color keep their values. Screens still pick tokens by name, so anything that used faint for text sits under 4.5:1 until KERNEL-576 moves screens to the role that fits. Amends KERNEL-431, which raised dark text on purpose. The dark neutrals stay pure grey, but ink-2, ink-3 and muted come down and faint becomes decoration. KERNEL-575

**The light ladder sits above the ratios KERNEL-574 asked for, because those targets can't all hold.** The parent asked for ink-2 near 9:1, ink-3 near 6.5:1 and muted at 4.5:1 or more on every surface. Hover (#e6e6e9) is 1.25x darker than the panel, so muted needs 5.6:1 on the panel to reach 4.5:1 there. With 1.35x between tiers, that puts ink-3 at 7.6 or more and ink-2 at 10.3 or more. The shipped light ratios on the panel are 17.6, 11.7, 8.2, 5.8 and 4.0, spaced about 1.4x to 1.5x apart. Dark has the same limit and lands on the targets: 17.6, 11.5, 8.3, 5.9 and 4.0. Light text keeps the slight cool cast it had. KERNEL-431 left light alone, and the light surfaces carry the same cast. KERNEL-575

**Input borders use a new token, `--input-line`, and `--line-2` stays a hairline.** WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for the border that tells you where an input is. `--line-2` has 66 uses, and most are card edges, table rules, tree guides and a meter track, which should keep their hairline. `--input-line` is #6a6a6a in dark and #82848a in light, and reaches 3:1 on every surface an input sits on (hover is left out, since it comes and goes). Inputs keep pointing at line-2 until KERNEL-576 moves them. The focus ring already reached 3:1 on every surface, so `--focus` keeps its values. KERNEL-575

**Increase contrast follows macOS through `@media (prefers-contrast: more)` in `tokens.css`.** Each text tier takes the value of the tier above it, and ink goes to full white or black. Dividers (`--line`, `--panel-line`) reach 2:1. Every other line reaches 3:1, except line-4 and the input border, which reach 4.5:1. The focus ring goes to 90% white or black. `test/theme.test.ts` checks every one of these, and that faint reaches 4.5:1 in this mode. KERNEL-575

**On text color, the token values win over the PNGs in `design/screens/`.** The PNGs keep the old greys and are not redrawn. They stay the spec for layout and copy. A shot compared with its PNG will show different text colors, and that is expected. This is the one exception to "the PNG wins" in CLAUDE.md and DESIGN.md. KERNEL-575
