**Create in the New chat modal always opens a new Lead tab.** `startLeadChat` used to take a Lead tab nothing had been sent in yet, and D-131 and D-133 kept that for the modal on the grounds that the user is the one starting the chat. In practice it broke two things. With an empty Lead tab open, the brief landed in that tab, so the tab count stayed the same and no new row appeared under the room in the sidebar. The tab row's + always adds a tab, so the two ways to start a chat disagreed. And the risk D-133 names for Ask Rowan applies to the modal too. The composer keeps unsent text in the renderer, so an empty tab may hold a draft, and taking it drops the brief into the middle of the draft and resets the tab's model, effort, plan mode and title. Now `startLeadChat` always adds a tab, and the `fresh` option is gone because every caller wanted what it did. Ask Rowan and Plan with Rowan behave as before. The floor brief, `lead.open` and "Let Rowan sort it" still use the first Lead chat. A room with no Lead workspace still ends up with one Lead tab after Create. `lead.start` makes the workspace and the brief's tab, and the `lead.open` the modal calls afterward finds that tab instead of adding an empty "Lead" one. The empty "Lead" tab `lead.open` makes when you open a room stays where it is, since you may be typing in it. Amends D-131 and D-133. KERNEL-242.
