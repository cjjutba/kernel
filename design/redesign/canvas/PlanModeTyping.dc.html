Here's what "remembers where you were" would cover:

  • Each workspace remembers its own open tab, and its open file and diff tabs.
  • Quit and reopen puts you on the same screen and tab. If that was archived or closed in the meantime, it falls back one level at a time (the workspace's first chat, then the room, then Home) without an error.
  • Drafts survive a quit.
  • Optional: ⌘[ and ⌘] to go back and forward.

How I'd split it

  1. Remember where you were during a session (Kai). Settings records the screen you came from, and Back to app and Esc return there. ⌘, reopens the last Settings page.
  2. Reopen where you left off (Kai, after 1 merges). Save the screen and each workspace's tabs, check them when the app starts using the fallbacks above, and save text drafts to disk.
  3. Back and forward (optional, Kai, after 1 merges). A history of screens in the store, with ⌘[ and ⌘] listed in Settings, Shortcuts.
