Kernel brand masters, Block K

Final masters for KERNEL-818. The icon K is unchanged from round 2. The wordmark K's channel narrows from 150 to 96 units, so "Kernel" reads as one word at 18 px. This sheet renders at 2x, so every size below is real pixels.

Wordmark at 18 px, the site nav size

Each lockup drawn at true 1x at four subpixel offsets (0, 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 px), enlarged 5x, then at real size. Round 2 splits into "I<ernel" wherever its 1.3 px channel lands on a whole pixel. The final channel is 0.84 px and never opens wider than the gap between the K and the e.
